Sandy Soils (Northern Savannah)

Prevalent across Kano, Kaduna, Katsina, Sokoto, and Jigawa. Low cation exchange capacity and fast water drainage.

  • Strategy: Split NPK and Urea into 2–3 smaller doses to prevent leaching.
  • Incorporate farmyard manure or compost every season.
  • Mulch heavily with millet or maize stalk residues to conserve topsoil moisture.
🧪

Acidic Soils (Southern Rainforest)

High rainfall in Lagos, Ogun, Edo, Delta, Rivers, and Cross River leaches basic cations, resulting in acidic pH (4.2–5.5).

  • Strategy: Apply agricultural lime (calcium carbonate) or dolomite at 1–2 tons/ha every 3 years.
  • Cassava and sweet potato tolerate lower pH better than maize or vegetables.
  • Rock phosphate or poultry manure helps unlock bound phosphorus.
🌿

Integrated Soil Fertility Management

Continuous synthetic fertilizer without organic matter degrades microbial soil health and hardens clay.

  • Rotate cereals with nitrogen-fixing cowpea, groundnut, or soybean.
  • Combine inorganic NPK with well-cured compost or kraal manure.
Northern Zone

Savannah & Sahel Region

Kano, Kaduna, Sokoto, Katsina, Bauchi, Borno, Niger
🌧️ Main Rainy Season (May/June – Sept/Oct):

Plant maize, sorghum, millet, upland rice, and groundnut upon consistent rainfall onset.

❄️ Harmattan Dry Season (Nov – March):

Arid winds and low humidity. Perfect for irrigated Fadama vegetable farming (tomatoes, onions, peppers, wheat).

Southern Zone

Humid Forest & Coastal Region

Lagos, Oyo, Ogun, Ondo, Edo, Rivers, Delta, Imo, Enugu
🌧️ Bimodal Rainfall Pattern:

Early Season (March – July): Ideal for early maize, cassava, yam, and vegetables.

August Break (short dry spell): Great window for harvesting early maize.

Late Season (Sept – Nov): Second planting window for late maize and cowpea.

Major Agricultural Hubs

Dawanau Market (Kano)

The largest grain and legume wholesale market in West Africa. Primary pricing hub for maize, cowpea, millet, and sesame.

Mile 12 Market (Lagos)

Premier distribution terminal for fresh tomatoes, peppers, onions, yams, and palm oil entering metropolitan Lagos.

Bodija Market (Ibadan)

Key transit market connecting Northern grain consignments with Southwestern distributors.

Post-Harvest Profit Maximization

  • Target Moisture: Dry maize and rice to 12–14% moisture before bagging in hermetic PICS bags to eliminate weevils without chemicals.
  • Early Morning Transit: Load and transport perishables between 8 PM and 6 AM to prevent thermal rot.
  • Value Addition: Processing raw cassava into dry garri, starch, or fufu extends shelf life from 48 hours to 12 months.
